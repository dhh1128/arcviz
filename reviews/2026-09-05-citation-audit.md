# Phase 5 citation audit

Auditor's stance: adversarial. The goal was to find citations that do not hold up, not to confirm the corpus is fine. This document reports what was found, including where the corpus held up well, since a clean-looking result needs the same evidence as a bad one.

## (a) Coverage

**Mechanical pass — all 223 records, complete.** Every record across the ten `refs/sources.*.yaml` files (acdc-spec 7, lit 16, oca 10, papers-acdc 40, papers-core 13, papers-identity 14, papers-intent 51, papers-other 14, w3c 40, wallets 18 = 223) was parsed and checked for: key uniqueness across files; required-field completeness (`quote`, `locator`, `supports` present unless `status: lead`); whether any `status: lead` record is cited in prose without being flagged as a lead; and whether every `capture:` path that points inside the repo exists on disk.

Citation-key resolution was checked by scanning all 15 files under `docs/research/**/*.md` for every backtick- or bracket-wrapped hyphenated token (161 candidates), cross-referencing against the 223 known keys. 142 resolved directly. The remaining 19 were individually inspected in context (not just pattern-matched) to rule out false negatives; all 19 turned out to be non-citation tokens — git branch names (`fix-worked-examples-schema-1520`, `add-e1e-edge-operator`, `ci-pages-step-strict`, `feat-indep-registry-bulk-issuance`), schema/field names (`sedi-id`, `v_k`, `primary_attribute`), a `VerdictState` enum value (`no-difference`), repo/package names (`entviz-js`, `arcviz-js`, `oca-bundle`, `cert-schema`, `vc-rem`), a skill name (`keri-review-panel`), and one illustrative example of the citation-key naming convention itself (`acdc-spec`, `cesr-spec`, `dossier-spec` used as examples in prose, not as live citations). Zero true dangling references found.

**Substantive pass — targeted, not random, per the brief.** Opened and verified at the stated locator:
- All 21 distinct source keys cited in `docs/research/disclosure-matrix.md` (the keystone document), which required reading roughly 15 distinct passages directly in the vendored ACDC spec (`~/code/me/kswg-acdc-specification/spec/spec-body.md`, lines 20, 91, 160, 164, 166–172, 661, 694, 1112–1116, 1165, 1197–1201, 1761, 1771, 1773, 1775, 1822) plus the `acdc-and-mtc`, `amp-diff`, `keri-primer`, `sdjwt-acdc`, `sign-author`, `telco-ev-reqs`, `wtbo`, `x509-prob`, `prog-a`, `wbca`, and `authenticity-vs-veracity` papers under `~/code/me/papers/`.
- Every citation in `docs/research/PLAN.md`'s Phase 3 "principles" section (the nine principle bullets, lines 118–132): five `amp-diff` keys, `eff-info-theory`, and three `intent-boundaries` keys, plus the `oia.md` and `~/code/me/papers/intent-boundaries.md` prose references.

That is roughly 30 distinct records opened against primary source text (several keys are shared between the two documents).

**Not reached.** The other 13 documents — `acdc-inventory.md` (~40 quoted spec passages), `corpus-keripy-examples.md`, `corpus-vlei-chain.md`, and all eight `prior-art/*.md` files — were checked only at the mechanical layer (key resolves, required fields present, capture exists) and were not re-opened against primary sources in this pass. That covers roughly 190 of the 223 records not substantively re-verified here. This is a deliberate scope choice per the brief's instruction to target load-bearing citations rather than sample randomly, not an oversight, but it means the accuracy demonstrated in the sampled ~30 records is not independently confirmed for the remaining ~190.

## (b) Mechanical failures

1. **`oca-v1_1_0-rc-wayback`** (`refs/sources.oca.yaml`), `status: primary`, has `supports` and `locator` but no `quote` field — a required-field violation of EVIDENCE.md rule 2. Note: the underlying citation is *not* broken in substance — it is quoted verbatim four separate times in prose (`docs/research/prior-art/oca.md:11,43,45,47`) against the local capture at `refs/captures/oca/oca-v1.1.0-rc-wayback.txt`, and all four quotes check out (verified below). This is a schema-compliance gap in the YAML record, not a bad citation.
2. **Eight `w3c` records** (`vcrm-github-repo`, `vcrm-ccg-repo-readme`, `vcrm-pr49`, `vcrm-pr49-comments`, `vcrm-pr30-oca`, `vcrm-issue7`, `vcrm-pr39`, `vcrm-issue1-blockcerts`) point `capture:` at `refs/captures/w3c/` but each says, in its own capture field, "not separately saved as a file." The directory exists; no dedicated capture artifact does. This is a self-disclosed tension with EVIDENCE.md rule 3 ("Public specifications and open-access papers are captured to `refs/captures/`") rather than a hidden one — the content is quoted in full inline in `prior-art/w3c.md` and `prior-art/oca.md` — but it means these GitHub-API-sourced quotes have no independently checkable artifact if the GitHub content changes or is deleted.
3. **Three cross-check records** (`acdc-spec-main-crosscheck`, `dossier-spec-main`, `cesr-spec-main-crosscheck`) describe themselves as fetched to `/tmp` for diff verification only, "not a citation source in its own right." Listed for completeness; by their own description they're out of scope of rule 3, not violations.

No duplicate keys anywhere in the 223. No dangling citation references found. No `status: lead` record cited as if it were a supported claim — all six lead-status citations found in prose (`doet_revised_2013`, `iso-18013-5-lead`, `iso-18013-7-lead`, `purchase1997aesthetic`, `sartor2022loveatfirstsight`, `tufte1983vdqi`) are correctly flagged "lead, not verified" or "not cited for any claim" at the point of citation.

## (c) Substantive failures, ranked

1. **[Moderate, load-bearing — PLAN.md Phase 3 principles, line 122]** The principle "Security-relevant state rides on discrete symbols, never on color" is argued as follows: *"`amp-diff.md:390` (Principle 5) argues from psychophysics that analog channels saturate near a few bits and leave an adversary a tolerance to hide in; the warning-and-indicator literature reaches the same verdict from user studies. Two independent lines of evidence, so this is settled before design starts."* The citation keys attached to the whole sentence are `[amp-diff-principle-5, amp-diff-no-color-alone, amp-diff-de76-trap]` — all three are the same `amp-diff.md` paper. The "warning-and-indicator literature reaches the same verdict from user studies" clause carries **no citation key at all**. The corpus does contain a warning/indicator cluster (`schechter2007emperor`, `dhamija2006phishing`, `felt2015sslwarnings`, `akhawe2013warningland` in `refs/sources.lit.yaml`, discussed in `docs/research/prior-art/literature.md:31,67`) that could plausibly be meant, but that cluster's actual finding is that *passive, easily-missed indicators fail to register with users*, and that behavioral adherence and comprehension are separable — a UX-attention claim. That is not the same claim as amp-diff's psychophysics point that *analog channels carry only a few bits and let an adversary hide inside the measurement tolerance*. The sentence asserts "two independent lines of evidence" converging on one verdict; only one line is cited, and the second, uncited line, even if identified, is arguably a different claim wearing the same conclusion. This is exactly the "supports goes further than the source" failure mode the audit was commissioned to hunt, compounded by an outright missing citation, in the section EVIDENCE.md treats as "stated as decisions."
2. **[Minor — PLAN.md line 119]** "COIA's unverified flag is the data-layer hook" is a factual claim about what a local codebase does, carrying no citation key. `docs/research/prior-art/local-codebases.md` does discuss COIA's flag machinery in comparable terms, so the claim is plausibly correct, but it is not linked to that discussion and EVIDENCE.md rule 1 requires a key for claims about what a shipped product does.
3. **[Minor, mechanical-adjacent]** See (b)1 — the `oca-v1_1_0-rc-wayback` missing-`quote` gap, noted here because the citation is genuinely load-bearing (it's the primary evidence for the report's strongest claim about OCA's markup surface — the Credential Layout Overlay's embedded CSS/font-loading example) even though the substance checked out.

No fabricated quotes were found anywhere in the sampled set. No instance was found, in the sampled ~30 records, of a quote being accurate while its specific `supports` field overstated it — the one genuine overreach found (item 1) is structural (a missing/mismatched second citation for a compound claim), not a per-record `quote`-vs-`supports` mismatch.

All spec-body.md line citations embedded in `disclosure-matrix.md` prose, confirmed verbatim against `~/code/me/kswg-acdc-specification/spec/spec-body.md` (branch `fix-worked-examples-schema-1520`, commit `651df33e`): lines 20, 91, 160, 164, 166–172, 661, 694, 1112–1116, 1165, 1197–1201, 1761, 1771, 1773, 1775, 1822 — including the more nuanced ones (the MAY-vs-MUST framing of edge validity propagation at 1112–1116, the AGID-vs-SAID distinction at 661, the metadata-ACDC non-commitment language at 172).

Note on `acdc-spec-vendored` specifically: its `supports` field names a broad sweep of ACDC content (field ordering, Attribute/Aggregate distinction, the full Edge and Rule sections, all seven Graduated Disclosure mechanisms, blinding mechanics, TEL message types), backed by one `quote` about field ordering. This looks structurally like the failure mode being hunted, but the record's own `locator` field explicitly defers the rest to `docs/research/acdc-inventory.md`'s ~40 individually-locatored quotes, and `disclosure-matrix.md`'s citation-discipline note (line 5) confirms this key is deliberately reused as a blanket pointer for spec-body.md claims whose specific line numbers are given inline in prose rather than in the YAML record. This is an unusual citation architecture (one key, many inline locators) rather than a violation, but it does mean this particular key's own `quote`/`supports` pairing should not be read as EVIDENCE.md rule 2 compliance in isolation — the actual verification burden sits in the ~15 spec-body.md passages checked above and in `acdc-inventory.md`, which was not independently re-verified in this pass (see Coverage).

PLAN.md principles-section keys: `amp-diff`, `amp-diff-walk-required`, `amp-diff-no-color-alone` (including the specific "design floor is ΔL* ≥ 20" figure, confirmed verbatim in `amp-diff.md:220`), `amp-diff-de76-trap`, `amp-diff-accessibility`, `eff-info-theory` (including the "33.32 bits" birthday+ZIP+gender figure — the YAML record's own `quote` field truncates before the gender clause, but the full captured source at `refs/captures/lit/eff-info-theory-privacy.html` supports PLAN.md's fuller figure), `intent-boundaries-definition`, `intent-boundaries-never-sneak`, `intent-boundaries-dont-imagine`.

## (e) Judgment

This corpus is trustworthy as a foundation for design work, with one correction needed before the affected principle is treated as settled. The keystone document, `disclosure-matrix.md`, held up completely under adversarial re-checking: every one of ~15 sampled spec passages matched the vendored spec text verbatim, line numbers were exact even for multi-hundred-line documents, and subtle distinctions (MAY vs. MUST in edge-validity propagation, AGID vs. SAID) were represented faithfully rather than smoothed over. The corpus also shows active self-policing — it flags its own tension between `acdc-and-mtc`'s "reconciled over time" framing and `keri-primer`'s "real-time status checks" framing rather than picking whichever reads better, and the EVIDENCE.md worked example about the original `amp-diff` mis-citation shows the failure mode this audit exists to catch was already caught once, by the corpus's own author, on a different claim.

The weakest spot found is `PLAN.md`'s principles section — the exact place the brief flagged as highest-priority because principles are "stated as decisions" — specifically the "two independent lines of evidence" claim in the discrete-symbols-not-color principle (finding (c)1). That claim should not be relied on as doubly-supported until either a real second citation is attached or the "two independent lines" framing is dropped. The second-weakest category is the eight GitHub-API-sourced W3C records with no persisted capture file: their content is quoted in full inline in the docs, so nothing is currently unverifiable, but they carry more link-rot risk than the rest of the corpus, which otherwise captures aggressively. Everything else found was a narrow, non-content-changing schema gap (the missing `quote` field on `oca-v1_1_0-rc-wayback`).

The larger caveat is scope: ~190 of 223 records, including all of `acdc-inventory.md`'s roughly 40 spec quotes and both corpus documents, were not re-opened against primary sources in this pass. Given how the sampled ~30 performed, I'd bet on them holding up, but "trustworthy corpus" as stated here is earned by the audited fraction, not by extrapolation over the whole.
