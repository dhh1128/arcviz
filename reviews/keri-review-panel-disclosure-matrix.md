## Executive verdict

The proposal is sound in KERI's own terms at the level that matters most: disclosure state genuinely is multi-axis, the refusal to collapse H/C/V/T/X into a single verified scalar is the correct reading of ACDC's graduated-disclosure model, and the anti-naive-lie posture behind Rules 2, 8 and 12 is well aimed. What does not hold up is the layer beneath it: several rendering rules assert values the substrate cannot supply, because the proposal's model of keripy's edge-operator dispatch and credential store is wrong at upstream/main e2a1e35c. Five HIGH findings converge on the edge/verification axis and should block - open question 5's "unknown operators render unevaluated, never invalid" is contradicted by the spec's default-injection MUST at spec-body.md:1197-1201 and by keripy in both directions (SEC-F1/KRT-F3/SPC-F2); Rule 9's effective operator is undeterminable whenever the far node is not in hand, which is the document's own normal case R1 (KRT-F1/SPC-F1); Rule 2b's four-valued outcome space has no fail that a keripy-fed host can ever assert for checks 1-4, 6 and 7 (SKP-F1); R0 treats reger.creds and reger.saved as one store when vc export enumerates the unvalidated one (SEC-F3); and the V axis is defined per-artifact while edges are verified per-edge, so no cell can carry the blocking SAID Rule 10 demands (KRT-F2). Three further HIGHs sit outside that cluster and are cheaper to fix: Rule 8 derives H2/H3 from a schema whose integrity the host interface never requires and whose u may be optional (SEC-F2, SPC-F3), expanding any attribute sub-block force-discloses the Issuee AID regardless of label-level accounting (PRV-F1), and chain-link-confidentiality terms never reach the render's reader (PRV-F2). The single thing to address first is the V axis: re-derive section B3, Rule 2 and Rule 9 against the actual dispatch at keripy src/keri/vdr/verifying.py:41,431-479, give V per-edge arity plus an explicit undetermined/unperformable state, and reopen question 5 - noting that SKP-F4 dissents, holding that conclusion right on a merely stale citation, so the disagreement needs adjudicating rather than counting lenses.
