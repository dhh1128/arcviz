SC6 — three absences that must not share a face (graph-led treatment)

Vocabulary used throughout: a place is a node whose content is in hand (a card). A hole (H7) is a named place whose content is not in hand — it has an address, so it gets node geometry. The frontier (H8 and every other region of unknowable topology) is a region, not a point: a blinded edge has no known far end, so nothing point-like may terminate it. A seal (H9) is a committed no-edge fact on a node's port strip — no hole, no frontier, no absence at all: a positive issuance-time fact. Cause is "unknown" everywhere below because no attestation channel is supplied (Rule 5). Nothing here fetches anything (Rule 17: static file).

Panel 4 — two blinded edges: convergence is unknowable, so it is undrawable

Fixtures two_blinded_edges_converge and two_blinded_edges_diverge are structurally identical by design — the ground truth differs, the disclosed bytes' shape does not. Rendering them differently would manufacture information. Under this design they render identically by construction: a blinded edge's far end is a region, not a point, so there is no coordinate at which convergence could be drawn or denied. The region's cardinality statement carries what is actually known.
