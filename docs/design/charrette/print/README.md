# Print-first: the charrette entry that designs the paper artifact first

Charrette seat: **print-first**. Files here: [sc6-hole-triptych.html](sc6-hole-triptych.html) (2 sheets), [sc2-production-chain.html](sc2-production-chain.html) (3 sheets), [sc3-diamond-depth.html](sc3-diamond-depth.html) (2 sheets), and [what-the-screen-adds.md](what-the-screen-adds.md). All mocks are standalone HTML+CSS, no build step, no external resources, no script; each is laid out for **A4 portrait (210×297 mm), 14 mm margins**, and the 182 mm content box prints unscaled on US Letter. Greyscale throughout — no colour appears anywhere, so "colour as pure enhancement" is satisfied vacuously.

## The premise, in one paragraph

Every other seat designs for the screen and degrades to paper; this one inverts that, because [principles.md](../../../research/principles.md) P10 says the inversion is not aesthetic: the property that makes verification state survive a habituated adversary — hard information riding discrete symbols and structure, never analog impressions — is the property that makes it survive monochrome, small size, and colour-blindness, and **print is the medium that enforces that honesty mechanically**. Paper has no hover, no toggle, no progressive disclosure, and no network, so Rule 17 passes by physics and every "available on demand" becomes "printed or absent." That forcing function surfaced the design language everything here uses: a **static-record strip** on every sheet (Rule 15/P15 — the artifact names itself a record, not a credential, and carries a hand-fill "printed on / by" line because the artifact cannot know its own print moment); a **section manifest** on every card, with no unmarked state, so absence is always a filled-in slot (the CATZOC move — see [precedent-for-absence.md](../../precedent-for-absence.md) §3 — applied to ACDC sections); **point-of-use captions** rather than a decoder-ring legend, because print pays no cost for words; a **verification record printed as a wall of empty and hatched boxes** (P3's honest default, given its full footprint on purpose); and an **identifier index** that enumerates byte-equal identifiers (P13.1 made always-on, since print has no "on demand"). The register throughout is *worksheet, not certificate*: ragged utility typography, no seals, no borders that flatter — a deliberate answer to P15's requirement that the static render carry less authority than a live one, and to the mDL "flash pass" failure the wallet audit found no shipped product addressing.

## Treatment per case

### SC6 — the hole triptych

Three absences, three different kinds of ink, and the differences are differences in *kind*, not in styling — following the one discipline that already solved this ([precedent-for-absence.md](../../precedent-for-absence.md) §1: Leiden keeps *lacuna* and *vacat* typographically distinct precisely so "nothing was ever here" cannot pass for "something was lost").

- **H7 (missing referent)** is a *lacuna with a name*: a real node position, drawn as a dashed-border box, with the blocking SAID printed inside it in full, "why it is not in hand: unknown — no cause was attested" (Rule 5), and every blocked check reporting unperformable-with-blocking-SAID in the record (Rule 10). Rendered from `corpus/loads/h7_missing_credential.json`, which produced a bonus fact the mock features: withholding ECR-AUTH *severs the chain*, so LE and QVI print as a second component joined to nothing, with a caption saying any connecting line would be manufactured (Rule 14, Rule 13's in-this-material scoping).
- **H8 (hidden destination)** gets **no box at all**: the committed edge is drawn as a line ending in a perpendicular terminus bar — no arrowhead, because there is nothing to point at — with the edge's own SAID and the words "its content, including where it points, is not in this material." A box, even an empty one, would be an invention; the deep H7/H8 distinction is *a place with a name* versus *no place*.
- **H9 (committed no-edge)** gets **zero graph ink**: the manifest slot reads "e: none as issued — committed," a positive statement in calm type. Because the manifest prints on every card, absence is a filled-in slot rather than missing ink, which is what keeps H9 from reading as a gap without needing any alarm treatment (Rule 1, Rule 13's committed-absence carve-out).
- **The composition**: `two_blinded_edges_converge` and `two_blinded_edges_diverge` print with byte-identical treatment — two terminus bars under one bracket labeled "destinations: one or two distinct artifacts — unknowable from this material" — and a caption stating outright that the drawn structure is identical *by design* because drawing the bars merged or pointedly apart would each manufacture a topology fact the disclosure withheld.

### SC2 — the production chain

Three sheets, deliberately: sheet 1 is the four-tier chain with the delegation-root hole; sheet 2 is the presenter frame and the verification wall; sheet 3 is the rules band, the identifier index, and the vocabulary recap. The load-bearing choices: all three edges print **identical** operator annotations ("operator (effective): I2I †") with one shared footnote, so the explicit-vs-default `o` difference is suppressed everywhere but raw bytes (Rule 9(a), pair 2). The delegation context is a **dotted line with no arrowhead** — a different kind of claim (host manifest, per `loads/h7_missing_delegator_kel.json`) than a committed edge, so a different line (Rule 14's three-edge-class separation). The root delegator's absent KEL is a dashed named hole with reach stated as "zero tiers" (Rule 10). The wall prints one row per (check, subject) — fifteen rows for this chain — with only two glyphs ever used (`☐` not performed, `▨` not performable from this material), a ✓ defined but never earned, ✗ absent because no host declared fail-capability (Rule 2 as amended), revocation phrased as horizon-bounded, and "no freshness policy supplied" as a visible state (Rules 3, 4). The recurring-AID alternation rides circled index marks plus full strings, resolved in the sheet-3 index (Rule 14, P13.1). The presenter question is a heavy frame *containing* the wall — the only route to the vector passes through it (Rule 18).

### SC3 — the diamond with depth

The shared ancestor prints **once**, at its deeper rank; all five edges print; the short path pays with a visibly long edge, annotated at the point a reader would wonder ("this line is long because the path is short — one hop, two ranks down"). The caption argues the two tree escapes in the artifact's own terms: duplicating A prints one SAID as two things and invites counting an invention; dropping an edge prints a disclosed relationship out of existence. The index surfaces two facts the drawing alone can't carry: A's SAID occurs three times (its `d`, and the `n` of both `baseline` edges), and B and F carry a **byte-identical edge block** (same edge SAID) — surfaced as equality, never as a similarity judgment (AF10). This mock is declared holder-facing, so no presenter indicator appears anywhere (Rule 18's foreclosure), which also lets the charrette set demonstrate both poles of SC10's split across its three artifacts.

## Self-assessment against the "fails if" lists

Stated plainly, failures first.

**Where I fail or cannot claim a pass:**

1. **SC6 — provenance leaks the composition's ground truth.** The fixture file names (`two_blinded_edges_converge.json` / `_diverge.json`) appear in sheet 1's produced-from line, and they name the very fact the render refuses to distinguish. The canvas treatment is identical, but the *document as a whole* lets a careful reader resolve the unknowable. I chose honest provenance over concealment and flagged it on the sheet itself; a reviewer may reasonably call this a failure of "rendering them differently manufactures information," one level up. The clean fix is host-side (opaque load labels), which is not mine to assume.
2. **SC6 — Rule 9(b)'s "operator undetermined" state is not instantiated.** The served H7 edge (`vlei_ecr`'s `auth`) carries an explicit `o: I2I`, so the mock shows "operator effective, relation uncheckable" but never the o-less-far-node-absent state whose honest render is "operator undetermined — far node unresolved." No corpus load produces that state today (it needs a recipe withholding `vlei_qvi` from `vlei_le`, a trivial G1 variant). The vocabulary is designed for it; the mock does not exhibit it.
3. **SC2 — the "renders as normal" observable is at genuine risk.** The occupancy is rendered without a single exceptional *word* — the strip says outright that this is what production traffic looks like — but the gestalt (three sheets, a heavy presenter frame, fifteen rows of empty boxes) may still read as a problem report to anyone habituated to certificates. I judge the words honest and the register defensible, and I also judge that a reviewer applying "any exceptional/degraded framing" strictly could fail it. The tension is real: SC2 demands *normal* and *incompleteness legible* simultaneously, and on paper, where nothing can be tucked behind disclosure, legible incompleteness is bulky.
4. **SC2/SC11 — the rules band is demonstrated with a specimen, not with fixture data.** The `vlei_*` fixtures carry no `r` section at all (gap G12), so the cards honestly print "r: none as issued — committed," and the 341–480-character layout problem is demonstrated by a self-describing 479-character sizing specimen labeled twice as not-credential-content. The layout commitment is shown (band below the fields, full-width, never truncated, data fields never squeezed); Rule 16's actual test — real chain-link-confidentiality prose travelling with the render — cannot be run against this corpus and is not claimed.
5. **Rule 15's diff test and P15's authority test are asserted, not run.** Both require a live render to diff against, and no live counterpart of these mocks exists. The static markers (strip, hand-fill line, "as of production" phrasing, historical-record wording) are designed to fail a chrome-identity diff, but that is a design intention, not a demonstrated pass.
6. **Full 44-character identifiers do not survive "being small."** At 100% they are the honest maximal form; below roughly 70% scale the strings blur before the marks, boxes, bars, and manifest words do. The structural vocabulary survives smallness; the identifier layer degrades to the circled marks plus the index, which is a real loss of glanceable identity (P8's cheap-rejection function weakens with it).
7. **SC3 — the AF1/pile observables are untestable here.** Paper has no focus interaction, so "raised card keeps its edges / occluded markers survive" is neither passed nor failed; the screen version must prove it separately. I record it as untested rather than discharged.

**Where I claim passes, with the observable:** SC6's three treatments are pairwise unconfusable in kind (named dashed box / terminus bar with no box / calm manifest text); no cause word appears anywhere with the cause channel empty, and the H8 note explicitly disowns "redacted/private/deleted"; nothing resembles loading or error because nothing on paper can be pending — though a screen-habituated reader might read a dashed box as a drop-target, the printed words foreclose it; the two-blinded-edges pair renders identically at the canvas level; no hole triggers network activity, by physics. SC2 shows four tiers plus the root hole with reach stated; nothing anywhere reads as a positive outcome without a host vector (no ✓ exists on any sheet); the `le`/`auth` operator difference is invisible outside raw bytes; identical AIDs print identically with identical marks. SC3 prints A once, all five edges present, both references to A's SAID byte-identical and indexed.

## Prominence budget accounting

T2's four budgeted states are stale, P-unknown (verifier-facing), the conditioned metadata marker, and H2-guessable. Spend per render: **SC2 spends exactly one** — P-unknown, as the heavy frame that contains the wall (structural placement, not a badge). Stale does not fire: no freshness policy was supplied, and "no policy supplied" renders as its own visible state in the record's footing, per Rule 4, without structural spend. The metadata marker does not fire: three fixtures have `u` absent (not empty) and the leaf's `u` is present, so no ambiguity trigger exists. H2-guessable is not in the occupancy. **SC6 spends one** (the same P frame, sheet 2). **SC3 spends zero** — holder-facing forecloses P, and no other budgeted state occurs. Everything else that is visually loud is either *topology* (the H7 boxes and H8 terminus bars occupy layout because the occupancy states are topology facts — shape-catalog I2 — not because they borrowed prominence) or *medium chrome mandated by Rule 15/P15* (the strip, which is a per-document constant). Under SC13-style multiplication the strip and frame do not multiply per card, which is this design's partial answer to HP3 — but the wall multiplies linearly with subjects, and I make no claim that it scales past a booklet.

## The strongest objection to this approach

**It buys honesty with bulk, and bulk is what trains dismissal.** The habituation literature this project already leans on (P9's evidence: Schechter's removed-indicator result, Felt's adherence-without-comprehension) says that a wall of repeated "not performed," met on every artifact every time, is exactly the stimulus people learn to stop seeing — so the design may be perfectly honest and functionally unread, failing HP2 not by lying but by boring. Three sheets for a four-credential chain is deposition-grade output; the screen seats will produce something people actually look at. The worksheet register has a second edge, too: forms *feel* official, so the register built to shed authority may quietly re-acquire it. And the sharpest version of the objection is the empty checkbox itself: ink invites ink. A reader can tick the boxes by hand and pass the paper on, manufacturing precisely the false "checked" record Rule 15 exists to prevent — the caption says "never from this page," but the affordance argues otherwise, and against a habituated reader the affordance usually wins.
