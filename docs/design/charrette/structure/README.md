Three standalone mocks, hand-authored, no build step, no external resources, no hue anywhere (each page is its own greyscale variant), nothing carried by hover, and a built-in small-size self-test strip (the whole board re-rendered at ~30%) at the bottom of every page:

- [sc2-production-chain.html](sc2-production-chain.html) — SC2, from `vlei_qvi`/`vlei_le`/`vlei_ecr_auth`/`vlei_ecr` plus `loads/h7_missing_delegator_kel`
- [sc3-diamond-depth.html](sc3-diamond-depth.html) — SC3, from the five `diamond_depth_*` fixtures

All SAIDs/AIDs in the mocks are the real corpus values (first 8 + last 4 characters); no credential data is invented.

## Premise

Security-relevant state rides structure or it is invisible (P9): the analog channels saturate at a few bits and leave an adversary a margin, and passive indicators go unnoticed no matter what carries them. So in this design **no epistemic state is ever a badge, chip, icon, or color** — if a state matters, it changes what the layout *is*. The unifying rule is that **space is commitment**: an allocated place means a committed, nameable identity; a drawn path means a committed edge; a place deliberately not allocated means the artifact withholds the destination; a sealed seam means absence committed at issuance; the enclosure of the whole render means render-level authority. A viewer who can see *where things are and where they are not* has already read the security state, before reading a single label — and an adversary cannot counterfeit a state without counterfeiting the geometry of the whole render, which is exactly the "disturb a picture, not a string" property P8 wants, applied to layout.

## The grammar (what each case exercises)

**SC6 — three absences, three geometric genera.** H7 is a *place without a thing*: a full-size dark socket that occupies the exact lattice slot the artifact would, displaces the layout accordingly, carries the referent's real SAID on its face, names every check it blocks (unperformable, blocking SAID named — Rule 10), and states "why absent: unknown; no retrieval attempted" (Rule 5, Rule 17). H8 is a *path without a place*: the committed edge leaves the card and crosses a heavy double boundary into a hatched **non-place band** where position carries no meaning; it terminates at a flat end-stop bar that is the terminus of the *edge* (labeled with the edge block's own digest — the one identity actually in hand), never a manufactured node. H9 is *no path at all, by commitment*: a solid black seam at the exact position where a sibling card has its edge-departure band — a wall the issuer built, worded as the issuance-time fact ("no edge section in this credential as issued", Rule 13), never as a fact about the world. The three differ in position (graph slot / page margin / card interior), scale (card-size / line / bar), and genus (mass / boundary-crossing / seal), so each pairwise distinction survives greyscale and thumbnail — checkable on the page's own 26% strip. For the two-blinded-edges composition, the honest move is to **destroy the metric where the fact is unknowable**: the band has no coordinates, so both edges end at edge-termini with no destination points that could visibly meet or avoid each other; `two_blinded_edges_converge` and `two_blinded_edges_diverge` render structurally identically, differing only in the digest strings — exactly as the artifacts do.

**SC2 — the normal case is a gate, a wall, and a dark foundation.** The verifier-facing render is entered through a **presenter gate**: a full-width double-bordered band the chain visibly hangs from, stating P = unknown before any V material is reachable (Rule 18); in a holder-facing render the gate does not exist and nothing marks its absence. Each card carries a flat, uniform, per-(check, subject) verification ledger whose steady state is the all-unperformed wall — rows fully occupied by their state words, with no empty boxes styled as if awaiting checkmarks (SC9's transience trap), fail not offered anywhere (no host declaration), staleness rendered as "no policy supplied", and unperformable rows naming their blocking identifier. The three chain edges — one explicit `I2I`, two defaulted — carry byte-identical plaque wording, "I2I — derived (far node … in hand, targeted)" (Rule 9(a), pair 2). The chain's root card ends in an H9 seam (the QVI credential has no edge section as issued), and below it the render pays for what it does not have: the **key-state foundation slab**, an H7 at the AID tier, naming the root delegator `EDYl3N_T…bK8-` from `loads/h7_missing_delegator_kel.json` and feeding the "unperformable" rows above it. Recurring AIDs (`EOvbsyiK…b48S` five times) render as identical pills (Rule 14).

**SC3 — the layout merges what the SAIDs merge, and nothing else.** A appears once, spanning both descent columns at its longest-path depth; B's edge to it simply travels further, passing F's tier as a visibly continuous wire. Path length is read by counting cards along a route, never by geometric distance — so unequal depths cost nothing but wire. All five edges are drawn, plaques threaded onto the wires; the two references to A carry identical `n` pills, and the two *byte-identical* `baseline` edge blocks (same edge SAID in B and F — a fixture fact) render identically, with no marker implying a comparison ceremony occurred (AF10, P8). All far nodes in this family are untargeted (no `a.i` anywhere), so every derived operator honestly reads `NI2I`, not the `I2I` a lazy default would print.

## Prominence rationing rule

Structure is allocated by the **ontological level of the state, never by its severity**, and each level owns one non-overlapping vocabulary:

- **Render-level** (axis P): the enclosure — the gate. At most one per render by construction (I5: P is per-render), so it cannot multiply. Cost: O(1).
- **Graph-level** (H7, H8, the delegation foundation): geometry — space allocated or refused. This multiplies only as the graph's own facts multiply, and each hole costs exactly the space of the fact it reports; a render with thirty holes is dominated by holes because the graph is, which is the honest picture, not a budget overrun. H8 spend is sublinear: any number of blinded edges terminate in the *one* shared non-place band.
- **Card-level** (T2's card-severity states): the card's own form, with disjoint deformations — H6 loses its frame entirely (issuer-uncommitted material is not card-shaped: an unframed sheet), stale breaks the frame (the perimeter interrupted by the expired as-of), and H2-guessable spends *space*: the guessable unit renders at disclosed-size as a transparent region, because effective publicness is a space fact in this grammar. Sketched here, not mocked — none of my three cases fires them. At most two form-states per card, so N cards stay countable.
- **Unit-level** (H2–H5 detail, V cells, T rows, operator vocabulary): **no structural spend at all.** These ride discrete symbolic form in flat ledgers and plaques — distinct per Rule 1 via shape and text (P9's capacity clause), deliberately *not* loud (they are outside T2's budget, and spending structure on them is the inversion T2 forbids — pair 2's suppression rule is the proof case).

This is my answer to HP3: the four budgeted states deform four different levels (enclosure, geometry, frame, space), so co-presence at scale never competes for one channel, and the only unbounded multiplier — unit states — is the one that costs nothing structural.

## Self-assessment against the fails-if lists

**SC6.** Pairwise distinctness of H7/H8/H9: pass (three genera, three positions; verified at 26% scale). Blocking SAID named, blocked checks unperformable: pass. H8 dropped or given a manufactured far node: pass. Cause words with empty channel: pass ("unknown" throughout). Convergence: pass by construction — but see failure (1) below. The `o`-less H7-far edge never shows "I2I (default)": pass — panel 3's control twin (`optional_edge_present`, `o`-less edge, far node absent) renders "operator: undetermined". Network on open: pass trivially (static page, zero external requests). **Failure (1), admitted:** the two-blinded-edges render necessarily contains two end-stop bars, because two committed edges have two line-ends; a naive reader may count bars and conclude "two destinations". The bars are defined as edge-termini and the band as positionless, but that definition is carried by a caption — i.e., at the weakest point of my own premise. I judge this the sharpest residual risk in the entry and do not know a geometry that removes it without lying in the other direction. **Failure (2), admitted:** the H7 socket's dark mass is designed to avoid loading/spinner/empty idioms (no dashes, no animation, no retry affordance, positive text), but whether users read it as "named absence" rather than "error" is exactly the kind of claim P9 says cannot be asserted from the armchair; it is untested and could fail the "resembles an error state" observable in a study.

**SC2.** Four tiers plus root delegator, reach named: pass. No positive outcome without a host vector, no fail glyph: pass. Default-vs-explicit `I2I` invisible outside raw view: pass (identical wording, checked by diff of the three plaques). Identical AIDs identical: pass. **Failure (3), admitted:** the four registry fixtures are rendered only as `rd` pills on their cards, not as drawn objects; the catalog lists them in SC2's fixture set, so this is an under-render — my defense (registries are TEL-tier and make no ACDC-layout demand) is I1-flavored but not adjudicated. **Arguable (4):** the brief forbids "exceptional/degraded framing", and the heaviest visual element of my normal render is a dark absence slab under a loud gate. My position is that these are the standing architecture of every verifier-facing render — they never toggle on "problems", so they *are* the normal frame and R1's warning is answered by making incompleteness the default look. A reviewer may still rule that the page as a whole reads degraded; if so, the failure is in my premise, not the execution, because the premise is precisely that honest normality looks like this.

**SC3.** A once, five edges, identical SAID occurrences identical: pass. **Failure (5), admitted:** the AF1/pile observable (raising a card must not detach A's edges or occlude siblings' markers) is asserted in a prose commitment on the page, not demonstrated — a static mock cannot pass an interaction test, and I did not build the interactive variant. **Failure (6), admitted:** to keep the topology legible, only the origin card carries its verification ledger on this page; B, C, F and A state theirs by reference to SC2's pattern. In a shipping render every card carries the wall, but as mocked, Rule 2's "unperformed must not be invisible" is under-demonstrated on four of five cards.

## Strongest objection to this approach

The grammar is a coined language with zero installed convention behind it. Sockets, seams, bands and gates must be *learned*; the literature my own premise leans on shows that interruptive treatments change behavior without producing comprehension (Felt, Akhawe), and it says nothing at all about users correctly parsing a bespoke layout grammar — so the honest reading of the evidence is that structure defeats habituation and counterfeiting, not that it communicates *which* state is present. An adapted-convention design gets first-glance familiarity for free; mine pays a training cost on every new viewer, and a print recipient gets no tutorial unless the export carries a legend (which then becomes chrome the issuer's canvas must not counterfeit — P11 — and one more thing to ration). Second, geometry is the one currency mobile does not have: paying honest space for every hole and every wall is exactly what SC13's smallest-viewport-times-largest-render case will punish, and my sublinear-band and flat-ledger answers are arguments, not demonstrations.
