# Charrette entry: precedent-led

Designer premise: start from [docs/design/precedent-for-absence.md](../../precedent-for-absence.md) and adapt, rather than invent. Six disciplines have spent decades or centuries drawing holes under arcviz's exact constraint — the artifact must not lie about what is known — and each of the treatments below is a named convention carried over, with the carrying-over defended. Where the survey's own negative finding bites (no discipline grades *the destination of a relationship*), the adaptation is flagged as invention wearing a borrowed coat, not passed off as transfer.

## The design vocabulary, and where each piece comes from

**The apparatus, everywhere (CATZOC + SRTM NUM).** Every render opens with a black render-apparatus band (P state, T state, scope), and every card carries a mandatory grading footer: the 9-cell V row, the T line, and an edge ledger (`e: 1 edge (auth)` / `e: vacat` / `e: section compact — count unknown`). This is [iho-s68-catzoc]'s core move: "unassessed" is itself a grade, mandatory, so no region of the render is ever ungraded and absence can never be achieved by leaving something blank. The V row is a channel physically separate from the content it grades — the SRTM NUM-file principle: the displayed value and its provenance tag never merge, and confidence is never inferred from how the value happens to look. Unperformed cells are *drawn* (outlined, dotted), never blank — the COIA/P3 rule that absence of a positive must be visible, in chart terms: an unsounded area is drawn as unsounded, not left white.

**H7 — the Leiden lacuna, with a counted identity.** A missing referent renders as a node-sized frame whose left and right borders are literal oversized square brackets, interior filled with tratteggio hatching (fine discrete strokes, detectable as intervention on any close inspection — [brandi-occelli-tradition]/[aic-code-ethics-art23]'s detectability obligation), containing the blocking SAID as a pill plus three fixed statements: what is missing, cause unknown (Rule 5), no fetch occurring or pending (Rule 17). The Leiden logic transfers exactly: this is a lacuna of *known extent* — we can count precisely what is missing (one artifact, this SAID) — so it gets the `[- ca. 5 -]` treatment, a bracketed, counted gap at a definite position in the line. It is never omitted (Rule 10; "no detectable stroke within restoration brackets" inverted: nothing known may be dropped into the gap either). In SC6 panel A the load recipe splits the served chain into two fragments, and the mock refuses to bridge them: whether the lacuna connects `le` to `ecr` is inside the missing artifact, so the lacuna gets dashed "own edges unknown" stubs instead of a manufactured bridge.

**H8 — a Glomar answer, drawn as unsurveyed margin.** A blinded edge is drawn full-length (dropping it redraws the topology — H8's naive lie), transitions from solid to widely spaced dashes, and terminates at a fixed-size double-bar gate on the boundary of a hatched *region* — the chart's "unsurveyed or inadequately surveyed area" [uschartno1] — inside which nothing is ever drawn. Three precedent constraints assemble this: (1) Glomar's structure ([phillippi-v-cia-1976]) — this is a different *kind* of answer from a redaction, not a stronger one, so it gets a different structural class (region) from H7 (place), never a darker version of the same node; (2) [lopresti-spitz-2005] — the gate glyph is fixed-size and the stub carries only what is actually committed (the edge's own SAID, its slot name), so nothing about the marker's geometry scales with what hides; (3) the unsurveyed area is a first-class charted object, not an absence of charting — the band is bounded, hatched, labeled. The `blinded_edge_group` variant (whole `e` section compact) gets a hatched in-card strip plus a single stub marked `{count: ?}` — one stub regardless of how many edges hide, for the same anti-leak reason.

**The convergence composition.** Both `two_blinded_edges_*` fixtures render as the same drawing: two edges, two identical gates, one shared region, and the region's label states the open question ("whether these destinations coincide is neither depicted nor deniable"). Because nothing is drawn inside the region, the render asserts neither convergence nor divergence; the treatment cannot encode the ground truth because no mark exists that could carry it. This is the covering argument in its cheapest form: identical-by-construction, not identical-by-discipline.

**H9 — the Leiden vacat, sealed and measured.** Committed absence renders *inside the card*, in the slot where an edge section would sit: a double-ruled, deliberately blank strip carrying the italic word *vacat* and the committed-by-the-enclosing-SAID statement, scoped per Rule 13 ("not a claim about the world"). The vacat/lacuna pair is the single most on-point convention the survey found — "nothing was ever here" and "something was here and is not" share a naive rendering (blank space) and the 1931 convention exists specifically to keep them apart — and the transfer adds a structural axis the print convention lacked: the vacat lives in card space (a fact about the artifact), the lacuna and the unsurveyed margin live in graph space (facts about the graph). The three treatments therefore differ in *where they are* before they differ in what they look like, which is what P9 means by state riding structure.

**SC2's frame (Vaughn index + ACR limitations section + LAPSED).** The production chain leads with a full-width, double-ruled `PRESENTER CONTROL: UNKNOWN` banner — the radiology move ([acr-communication-practice-parameter-2025]): a mandatory, separately-labeled report component that states what the study could not answer, placed before findings (Rule 18's ordering made structural). Each card's per-cell grading with named blockers (`5 unperformable † root delegator KEL EDYl3N_T…`) is the Vaughn-index posture: withholding itemized per unit, in situ, never asserted once for the whole document. The delegator hole is a lacuna at AID granularity, connected by a dotted no-arrowhead "reported-by-context" stroke that is visibly not an ACDC edge (Rule 14's three edge classes). And the whole render carries no warning chrome: R1 is the normal case, and the footer says so in words ("this render is normal, and it says so by saying nothing special").

**SC3 (Venice Charter, anastylosis).** One committed artifact, one node: A is drawn once and the B→A edge simply runs longer, because both tree escapes are the forgeries the Charter forbids — duplicating A fabricates a second original; dropping an edge falsifies the record for every later viewer. The three occurrences of A's SAID (A's `d`, B's and F's `n`) wear one identical pill face, with no connecting line or glow: identical rendering surfaces the correlation (Rule 14, P13.1) without implying a comparison ceremony ran (P8, AF10). Path-length difference is carried purely by edge routing and stated only in the mock's caption — a derived quantity gets no badge (pair-2 logic).

## Self-assessment against the "fails if" lists

**SC6.** *Pairwise confusability (Rule 1):* pass, and by structural class rather than styling — H7 is a bracketed place, H8 a hatched region, H9 a sealed in-card strip; each pair differs in position and form simultaneously. *H7 names its blocking SAID / checks report unperformable:* pass (pill in the frame; †-noted cells). *H8 dropped or given a manufactured far node:* pass (edge always drawn; no node outline ever appears at its end). *Reads as loading/error:* **partial risk, honestly held** — the hatched lacuna with a grey frame lives in the same visual neighbourhood as a skeleton-loader, and the defense (bracket glyphs plus the "no fetch occurs or is pending" line) leans on prose. At card scale I judge it distinct; at 100 px the brackets are all that survive, and brackets are not a web convention. This is the trained-reader problem, admitted below. *Cause word with empty cause channel (Rule 5):* pass — "cause: unknown" everywhere; the H8 stub deliberately refuses "withheld"/"private," saying only "not in this presentation." *Two H8 edges drawn as definitely converging or separate:* pass by construction (nothing drawn inside the region); but see objection 2 — the *naive read* of one shared band may lean toward convergence, and the counterweight is a text label. *`o`-less H7-far edge showing "I2I (default)":* pass — exercised on `optional_edge_present`'s endorsement edge, rendered "op: undetermined — o unspecified, far node unresolved"; the one H7-adjacent edge with an explicit `o` (`vlei_ecr`'s `auth`) renders "stated in edge (explicit)," which is determined and honestly so. *Opening a hole triggers network activity:* pass trivially (static), and stated in the treatment.

**SC2.** *Fewer than four tiers / root delegator omitted / delegation without reach:* pass — five graph objects drawn, the fifth being the delegator lacuna; reach carried as "unperformable past the KELs held," †-named. *Positive check outcome without a host vector:* pass — 0 of 36 cells performed, no pass glyph exists in the file, no fail glyph either (Rule 2c: none declared). *Default-vs-explicit `I2I` visible outside a raw view:* pass — the three edge chips are worded identically; the syntactic difference is described only in a caption explicitly marked "mock annotation, not render chrome." *Identical AIDs rendering non-identically:* pass — `EOvbsyiK…48S` wears one pill face in all three positions. *Exceptional/degraded framing:* pass in chrome; **at risk in gestalt** — a wall of 36 quiet cells plus a bracketed hole plus a heavy P banner may *feel* degraded to an untrained viewer even though nothing says so. Whether "normal" can survive that much visible incompleteness is exactly HP2, and this mock does not solve HP2; it renders it.

**SC3.** *A appears twice:* pass. *Any of five edges absent:* pass. *Two occurrences of A's SAID rendering non-identically:* pass. *Focus/pile interaction detaching a raised card:* **not demonstrated** — these are static mocks and AF1's pile constraint is an interaction property; nothing here shows the raised-card behaviour, so this observable is unmet rather than failed. Noted as the entry's scope boundary.

One further judged risk, cross-case: the full-width vacat strip spends structural weight on a state outside T2's prominence budget (H9's naive lie is "hole" or "none exists," not "fine"). My defense is that the strip is *quiet* structure — a sealed blank, no alarm — so it buys distinctness without spending loudness; a judge may still call it budget inversion.

## Prominence: spent and withheld

Spent: the P-unknown banner (SC2 — the loudest object in any mock, per Rule 18 and T2); the render-apparatus band (always); the two graph-space absence structures (lacuna, unsurveyed margin), which are large because they are *topology*, not because they are alarms. Deliberately quiet: the all-unperformed wall (small dotted cells with a one-line count — true, present, and calm, because it is the steady state and must not train dismissal); the T no-policy line (visible state, one line); committed absence (sealed blank, the quietest of the three absences by design — it is the only one of the three that is actually resolved knowledge); operator provenance ("derived" in chip text, never a badge — pair 2 demands suppression, not decoration).

## Strongest objection to this approach

It is an apparatus criticus, and apparatus assumes a reader. Every discipline this entry borrows from openly relies on trained readers and a key — the survey documents this as a limit, not a footnote ([tei-mahoney-epigraphy]: publishers omit the complicated markings for beginners; [yale-jreg-glomar-opaque]: even the *name* of the third state needed a plain-language gloss; and CATZOC's own guide records that mariners switch the confidence layer off during the highest-stakes phase). The English glosses written into every treatment are this entry's compensation, but they make prose load-bearing at exactly the small sizes where prose dies first — and if arcviz's untrained verifier habituates past the apparatus the way mariners do, the render is honest in exactly the way a fully-marked epigraphic edition is honest: correct, complete, and unread. A second, sharper form of the same objection: brackets, hatching, and an italic *vacat* radiate scholarly care, and that aesthetic is itself an unearned trust signal (the korir2022wallet failure, imported through the back door). The from-first-principles entries will not have this problem; whether they can match the triptych's three-way structural separation without a century of borrowed convention is the comparison this charrette exists to make.
