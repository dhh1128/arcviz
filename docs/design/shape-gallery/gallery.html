arcviz — rough shape gallery

Placeholders, not a design. One treatment (compression to a floor, budgeted states floored larger) applied to seven DAG shapes at 360 px, so the shapes and densities can be compared by eye instead of by description. Identifier prefixes are real corpus values. Deliberately unfinished: no type hierarchy, no spacing refinement, no state vocabulary beyond what each shape needs to be legible as a shape.

One card, expanded. No graph chrome earned.

▪ = committed absence (a positive mark, not blank space)

2 · Chain, depth 2

Both at the floor; edge drawn between them.

Floor chip = 76×44. Carries identity and one state; not parameters.

3 · Production chain, depth 4 — with a hole at the root

The normal case: fully disclosed, still incomplete.

The hole has an address — it occupies a real position and names what is missing.

4 · Diamond with depth

One ancestor, two path lengths. Not a tree.

Duplicating A or dropping either edge would both be lies; the path-length difference is the point.

5 · Fan-out — one node, several children

Width, not depth. Chips pack; the parent expands.

Four chips fit the width; a fifth wraps. This is where the floor earns its keep.

6 · Two blinded edges — destination unknowable

The edges are committed; where they point is not.

7 · Mixed states — the second floor
