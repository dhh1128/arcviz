Reading: the double border survives every step. The hatch degrades asymmetrically, because texture legibility depends on area: by 0.55 the hole's small interior reads as flat grey while the larger frontier swatch still reads visibly striped — so the "one texture, one concept" link between hole interior and frontier is broken at chip scale even before the texture itself disappears, and a viewer comparing a chip-sized hole against the region has no texture match to find. From that point the hole/frontier distinction is carried entirely by bordered-and-pilled vs unbordered, plus position. The slab fallback is therefore not triggered, but the hatch's job description shrinks to "large-scale texture only", and the pill inside the hole is illegible below 0.72 even though the border is not.
