COMMENTARY — OUTSIDE THE RENDER

Same host stubs as variant A (declared in that file's commentary and in README.md). What moving the ledger bought and cost, measured on this pair of files: the graph column shrinks from 6,710 px to 4,110 px and the whole render from 6,884 px to 4,858 px (measured headlessly), with the 573 px matrix block compressing all 101 (check, subject) cells into less than one phone screen without collapsing arity. What it costs: every Rule 10 blocking identifier moved out of the cell into footnotes a–f, so "why is this cell ⊘" is now a three-hop read (card → column → footnote list) where variant A answers it in place; the hole elements can no longer name their blocked checks directly and point at ledger notes instead; and once the viewer scrolls past the matrix, no V state of any kind is co-visible with any card — the manifest's "V: ledger col NN" mark is the only surviving trace, and it is a pointer, not a state.

Honest observation in the other direction: at the all-unperformed default the per-card ledgers of variant A are eleven near-identical blocks whose card-specific content is only the blocker pills — the matrix makes the uniformity visible as a fact (three text-heavy footnotes carry everything that actually differs), where variant A makes the viewer discover it by reading eleven times.
